want need investment problem for people with high money needs...

A person earning 10k/month is having 
5k room rent and 2k food of one month.

so here the 50% wants 30% needs and 20% investment rule cant be applied.

_It's obvious that a person with 10k salary may not have a laptop or a personal computer_

Let's have another example 

A person earning 35k /month has his own home.

but is running home loan of 20k/month in monthly installments (because s/he wished s/he had a home bigger than her dreams can afford).

Now here again the Wants needs and investment rule cant be solved.

so the program is intended to solve the similar problem.
